Crafting

Who in the guild has which profession, and what they can make. Open a profession's recipes and hover a recipe or a material for its tooltip.

Skill levels come from the Armory. A recipe taught by a trainer lists everyone whose skill is high enough for it. A recipe that drops, is sold or comes from a quest lists a character only once the admin has ticked them. Recipe data and tooltips: Wowhead. Leveling guides: WoW-professions.
